# Community Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful to creators and contributors from different languages, skill levels, and hardware backgrounds.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Credit original creators. Do not harass submitters, post private information, misrepresent another person's work, or pressure anyone to Star the repository in exchange for publication.

Maintainers may edit or remove spam, plagiarism, unsafe instructions, personal attacks, or content that cannot be attributed. Serious or repeated violations may result in participation restrictions.

For ordinary moderation concerns, open a focused Issue. For sensitive safety or privacy concerns, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
