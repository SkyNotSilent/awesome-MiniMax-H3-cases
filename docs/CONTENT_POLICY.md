# Content and Rights Policy

The MIT license covers this repository's original code and project documentation. It does **not** relicense third-party videos, Prompts, names, screenshots, posts, model weights, or linked materials.

## What the catalog publishes

- A playable case only after its creator/source and MiniMax H3 relevance can be checked.
- Prompt text only when the complete text is publicly disclosed by the creator or an official source. The catalog preserves it verbatim and links the evidence.
- Tutorials combine source-attributed structured guidance with clearly marked captured source material. Captured originals retain their authors' rights.
- Local media copies for reliable site playback while keeping the original-source link and a removal path.

## Corrections and removal

Creators and rights holders can request attribution correction, account merging, media removal, or complete removal through the focused Issue templates. Disputed content may be hidden while the request is reviewed.

This community project is not affiliated with MiniMax. Source-platform and upstream-project terms continue to apply.

## Advertising eligibility

Source attribution and public availability do not establish permission for commercial reproduction. Before a page is added to advertising inventory, its reproduced videos, audio, text, posters and images need a rights check, and the page must provide meaningful original commentary or curation. A model's commercial license does not grant rights to another creator's work. Only explicitly approved pages can display advertising; rights questions keep a page out of that inventory.
