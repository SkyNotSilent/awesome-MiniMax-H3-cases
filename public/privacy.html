账号与收藏隐私说明

Account and saved-case privacy

我们保存什么、为什么保存

公开浏览无需账号。使用 Google 登录后，本站保存 Google 账号标识、邮箱、姓名、头像地址及收藏，用于识别账号和跨设备同步收藏。本站仅申请基本身份、邮箱和个人资料权限，不保存 Google access token 或 refresh token。

登录会话最长有效 30 天，退出会撤销当前会话。会话令牌仅保存在 HttpOnly Cookie 中，服务端保存令牌摘要。账号与收藏存储在独立的应用数据库中，不加入站点流量统计。

旧收藏与数据删除

首次登录会自动将当前浏览器旧收藏合并到账号中。导入成功后清除本机旧收藏；导入失败保留本机数据。旧 Railway 域名与当前域名的浏览器存储分别独立，需要在旧站登录同一个 Google 账号导入。

账号资料和收藏保留至你请求删除。需要删除时，请使用下方私密联系入口，工作人员会先验证账号归属。删除账号会一并删除其会话、收藏和导入记录。请勿在公开 Issue 中提交邮箱、登录凭据或个人收藏。

What we store and why

Public browsing needs no account. After Google sign-in, we store your Google account identifier, email, name, avatar URL and saved cases to identify your account and sync favorites across devices. We request only basic identity, email and profile scopes and do not persist Google access or refresh tokens.

Sessions last up to 30 days. Signing out revokes the current session. Session tokens are kept in HttpOnly cookies and hashed on the server. Account data is stored in a separate application database and is not included in traffic analytics.

Old saved cases and deletion

First sign-in merges this browser's old saved cases into your account. Browser data is cleared only after a successful import and retained on failure. The old Railway host has separate browser storage; sign in there with the same Google account to import its favorites.

Account information and favorites remain until you request deletion. Use the private contact link below. We verify account ownership before removing its sessions, favorites and import receipts. Do not post your email, credentials or personal favorites in a public Issue.
