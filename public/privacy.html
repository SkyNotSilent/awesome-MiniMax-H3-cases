隐私说明

Privacy policy · Accounts, favorites and advertising

我们保存什么、为什么保存

公开浏览无需账号。使用 Google 登录后，本站保存 Google 账号标识、邮箱、姓名、头像地址及收藏，用于识别账号和跨设备同步收藏。本站仅申请基本身份、邮箱和个人资料权限，不保存 Google access token 或 refresh token。

登录会话最长有效 30 天，退出会撤销当前会话。会话令牌仅保存在 HttpOnly Cookie 中，服务端保存令牌摘要。账号与收藏存储在独立的应用数据库中，不加入站点流量统计。

旧收藏与数据删除

首次登录会自动将当前浏览器旧收藏合并到账号中。导入成功后清除本机旧收藏；导入失败保留本机数据。旧 Railway 域名与当前域名的浏览器存储分别独立，需要在旧站登录同一个 Google 账号导入。

账号资料和收藏保留至你请求删除。需要删除时，请使用下方私密联系入口，工作人员会先验证账号归属。删除账号会一并删除其会话、收藏和导入记录。请勿在公开 Issue 中提交邮箱、登录凭据或个人收藏。

What we store and why

Public browsing needs no account. After Google sign-in, we store your Google account identifier, email, name, avatar URL and saved cases to identify your account and sync favorites across devices. We request only basic identity, email and profile scopes and do not persist Google access or refresh tokens.

Sessions last up to 30 days. Signing out revokes the current session. Session tokens are kept in HttpOnly cookies and hashed on the server. Account data is stored in a separate application database and is not included in traffic analytics.

Old saved cases and deletion

First sign-in merges this browser's old saved cases into your account. Browser data is cleared only after a successful import and retained on failure. The old Railway host has separate browser storage; sign in there with the same Google account to import its favorites.

Account information and favorites remain until you request deletion. Use the private contact link below. We verify account ownership before removing its sessions, favorites and import receipts. Do not post your email, credentials or personal favorites in a public Issue.

广告、Cookie 与第三方

本站准备使用 Google AdSense 展示广告。广告仅在启用后加载；Google 及其他广告供应商可能使用 Cookie、本地存储、网络信标、IP 地址和设备标识，依据你对本站或其他网站的访问展示广告、衡量效果及防止无效流量。广告供应商可能在浏览器中放置和读取 Cookie。本站不向广告配置传入邮箱、账号标识或个人收藏。

适用地区使用 Google 的同意管理提示。你可以通过“广告隐私选择”重新管理选择，也可以访问 Google 广告设置管理个性化广告，或通过 第三方退出入口管理参与广告网络的选择。Google 如何使用合作网站的数据说明 Google 的处理方式；其他供应商及其选择入口由同意提示列出。

站点统计与必要存储

本站使用自托管 Umami 了解页面访问和功能使用情况；账号信息和个人收藏内容不加入统计事件。语言偏好、登录会话和收藏迁移记录用于提供网站功能。广告选择不影响浏览或 Google 登录收藏。

Advertising, cookies and third parties

This site is preparing to use Google AdSense. Advertising loads only after it is enabled. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, IP addresses and device identifiers to serve ads based on visits to this or other websites, measure performance and prevent invalid traffic. Advertising vendors may place and read cookies in your browser. We do not pass email addresses, account identifiers or personal favorites into our ad configuration.

Google consent messages apply in relevant regions. Use “Ad privacy choices” to revisit your selections, Google Ads Settings to manage personalized advertising, or the industry opt-out page for participating networks. Read how Google uses data from partner sites. Other vendors and their choices are listed in the consent message.

Site analytics and necessary storage

Self-hosted Umami helps us understand page visits and feature use. Account information and personal favorite contents are excluded from analytics events. Language preferences, sign-in sessions and favorite import records support site features. Advertising choices do not restrict public browsing or Google sign-in and favorites.
