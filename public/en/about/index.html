About this site

MiniMax H3 Cases & Guides is an independently maintained community library. It helps readers compare video examples, find complete prompts published by their creators, and choose setup and creation guides suited to their hardware.

How we organize resources

Examples retain their original sources and are organized by generation mode, scene and style. Guides include requirements, prerequisites, steps, checks and known limitations. Source material and our editorial guidance are identified separately. An author's benchmark is not a generation test performed by this site.

We publish prompts only when the creator makes the complete text available. Unpublished prompts are never inferred, completed or rewritten. References support traceability and do not imply endorsement.

Use examples in your own work

Choose a mode that matches your input assets, then select examples by scene and style. Check subject consistency across cuts, camera continuity, and sound timing against picture events. Turn these observations into a checklist for your own output.

Case summaries identify what to watch, while creator-published prompts stay verbatim. Resolution and duration describe the output; speed and VRAM needs require checks on your own hardware. Tutorials put our reading advice or execution notes before the attributed original and identify source checks and site testing separately.

Updates and corrections

Cases retain their original links and first inclusion dates; guides retain source-check dates. Source versions, filenames and benchmarks describe the configuration at that time, so consult current project instructions before running them. Guides awaiting review are marked, excluded from core selections and show no ad unit. Authors and readers can request corrections or removal through Contact and copyright.

Who maintains the site

Maintainer: SkyNotSilent. This is not an official MiniMax or ComfyUI website and is not officially affiliated with the cited creators or services.

Advertising and content
